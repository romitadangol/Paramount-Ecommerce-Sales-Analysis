
# Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Sales Performance

The business generated NPR 1,673,400 in recorded product sales during the reporting period (April 2022 – October 2026).

**Insight:** The sales data provides a baseline for monitoring business performance over time.

**Recommendation:** Track monthly sales consistently and compare performance across periods to identify changes in demand and seasonal patterns.

## 2. Product Category Performance

Paints & Art Supplies was the highest-contributing category, generating NPR 693,226 in recorded product sales.

**Insight:** Art supplies represent a significant portion of the business's recorded sales.

**Recommendation:** Monitor stock availability for popular art supplies, evaluate product-level demand, and explore opportunities to expand complementary products.

## 3. Product Performance

The Colorful Plastic Wire Clothing Hanger Set (12 Pcs) was the highest-revenue individual product, generating NPR 249,475 across 1,577 transactions.

**Insight:** Products outside the traditional stationery range can also contribute substantially to overall sales.

**Recommendation:** Evaluate the profitability and demand of high-volume products before making inventory and promotional decisions.

## 4. Monthly Sales Trends

- Highest monthly sales: NPR 61,717 (November 2022).
- Lowest monthly sales: NPR 10,215 (May 2022).

**Insight:** Monthly sales varied throughout the reporting period.

**Recommendation:** Investigate sales drivers during stronger and weaker months, including promotions, product availability, and seasonal demand.

## 5. Financial Transactions

Total recorded net income-statement transactions amounted to NPR 1,373,120.64.

**Insight:** Sales revenue and recorded net income-statement transactions differ because the latter includes fees, refunds, shipping-related transactions, and other adjustments.

**Recommendation:** Monitor major fee and deduction categories regularly and compare recorded settlements with actual seller payouts before assessing business profitability.

## 6. Overall Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize inventory monitoring for high-demand products.
2. Review category-level sales performance regularly.
3. Investigate monthly sales fluctuations to identify seasonal opportunities.
4. Monitor platform commissions, payment fees, and shipping-related deductions.
5. Evaluate product profitability using actual product costs and other business expenses.
6. Develop recurring sales dashboards to support ongoing business decisions.

## Conclusion

This analysis demonstrates how historical e-commerce sales and financial transaction data can be transformed into structured business insights. The findings provide a foundation for better sales monitoring, inventory planning, and financial oversight.
