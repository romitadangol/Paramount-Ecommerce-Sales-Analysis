
# Paramount Ecommerce Sales & Financial Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a real-world e-commerce sales and financial analysis of Paramount Stationery & Sports, a retail business selling stationery, school supplies, art materials, sports products, toys, and home organization items through Daraz.

The project uses Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, product and category contributions, monthly trends, and recorded financial transactions. An interactive executive dashboard was developed to present key business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Understand monthly sales trends and fluctuations.
- Examine recorded income-statement transactions, fees, and deductions.
- Develop an executive dashboard to communicate business insights.
- Identify opportunities for improving business performance.

## Tools & Technologies

## Dataset Overview

The analysis uses historical Daraz order and income-statement exports covering April 2022 to October 2026.

The datasets include product-level sales information, transaction dates, product categories, and financial transaction types.

Confidential transaction and customer-level information is not included in the public repository.

## Key Areas of Analysis

1. **Sales Analysis:** Overall sales performance and revenue trends.
2. **Product Analysis:** Product-level sales contributions and rankings.
3. **Category Analysis:** Comparison of sales across product categories.
4. **Monthly Analysis:** Monthly sales and recorded net settlement trends.
5. **Financial Analysis:** Examination of commissions, payment fees, shipping charges, refunds, and other recorded transactions.
6. **Executive Dashboard:** Visual presentation of key performance indicators and business insights.

## Key Findings

- Total recorded product sales: NPR 1,673,400.
- Total recorded net income-statement transactions: NPR 1,373,120.64.
- Highest monthly product sales: NPR 61,717 in November 2022.
- Lowest monthly product sales: NPR 10,215 in May 2022.
- The analysis covers multiple product categories, including paints and art supplies, stationery, sports items, toys, and home goods.

## Financial Interpretation

The recorded net income-statement transactions represent the sum of the transaction types included in the analysis. This figure should not be interpreted as final seller payout, bank settlement, or business profit.

Product-level and monthly financial summaries may also differ in scope where transactions are not associated with individual products.
